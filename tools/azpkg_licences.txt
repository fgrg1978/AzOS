# The licences an AzOS application package may carry (tools/azpkg.py lint).
#
# One SPDX identifier per line. An expression passes when every AND operand and
# at least one OR operand is listed here; `X WITH exception` counts as X.
# The list is permissive licences plus MPL-2.0 (file-level copyleft that does
# not reach the rest of an image). Copyleft that would bind a whole image (the
# GPL family, AGPL, LGPL linked statically, as every Rust program here is) is
# not on it: an author who has such a component under an OR choice lists it
# in the expression, and one who has it alone gets a refusal at build time and
# can ask for an addition here by a reviewed change, or pass `--allow ID` to
# azpkg for a private build that no device policy has to accept.
